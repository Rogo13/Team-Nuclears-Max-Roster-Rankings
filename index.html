💡 Ranking Formulas

Base Damage Function:
damage = floor(0.5 × power × (atk ÷ def) × effectiveness × STAB × weather) + 1
STAB = 1.2 if move type matches attacker's Pokémon type, else 1.0
weather = 1.2 if move type is boosted by current weather, else 1.0
effectiveness = type multiplier (0.39, 0.625, 1.0, 1.6, 2.56, etc.)

Weather Boost Reference:

💨 WindyDragon, Flying, Psychic

Team Size Multiplier:
teamMultiplier = ceil(100 × f1 ÷ ((TIT - 1) × f1 + 0.5)) ÷ ceil(100 ÷ TIT)
where: f1 = your Pokémon's fast move duration (seconds), TIT = trainers in team (1-4)

⚔️ Attacker Score (higher is better):
effectiveMaxLevel = cannonActive ? min(maxLevel + 1, 4) : maxLevel
effectivePower = MAX_POWER[maxType][effectiveMaxLevel]
attackerScore = damage(effectivePower, yourAtk, bossDefense, effectiveness, STAB, weather)

🔵 Shielder Score (lower is better):
avgChargedMoveDmg = average of damage(bossMoves, bossAtk, yourDef, effectiveness, bossSTAB, weather)
effectiveGuardLevel = cannonActive ? min(maxGuard + 1, 4) : maxGuard
levelAdjustment = {0:∞, 1:1.67, 2:1.33, 3:1.0, 4:0.67}[effectiveGuardLevel]
shielderScore = avgChargedMoveDmg × levelAdjustment × teamMultiplier

🛡️ Tank Score (lower is better):
avgChargedMoveDmg = average of damage(bossMoves, bossAtk, yourDef, effectiveness, bossSTAB, weather)
effectiveHP = yourHP (Zamazenta gets: yourHP + guardLevelBonus)
tankPct = (avgChargedMoveDmg ÷ effectiveHP) × 100
tankScore = tankPct × teamMultiplier

💚 Healer Score (higher is better):
effectiveSpiritLevel = cannonActive ? min(maxSpirit + 1, 4) : maxSpirit
healPct = {0:0, 1:0.08, 2:0.12, 3:0.16, 4:0.20}[effectiveSpiritLevel]
healerScore = floor(yourHP × healPct)

Notes:
• Dynamax Cannon boosts all levels by +1 (except Zacian/Zamazenta)
• Guard Lv0 → excluded from Shielder rankings
• Spirit Lv0 → excluded from Healer rankings (unless cannon boosts to Lv1)
• Weather boost applies to both your max moves AND boss charged moves
• Zamazenta (Crowned Shield): Gains bonus HP for Tank scoring only
  Guard Lv0/1/2/3/4 = +0/20/40/60/80 HP (e.g., 207 base HP + 60 = 267 HP at Guard Lv3)
